A Short Synthetic Report on Melatonin and Sleep Onset
Probe Author

Abstract
Deliberately short fixture: real text, but below the two-thousand-character
threshold.

Methods
Fourteen children received melatonin and fifteen received placebo; sleep
was recorded with diaries for one week.

Results
Sleep onset latency was 15.0 +- 6.0 min in the melatonin group (n = 14)
and 21.5 +- 7.5 min in the placebo group (n = 15).

Discussion
Synthetic values. Not evidence.
